Unlike Buckinghamshire's single National Offer Day distance published for every school on one common measure, the Slough Consortium's four schools don't rank on distance alone — each applies its own layered oversubscription criteria first (a catchment radius, postcode Priority Areas, a faith-category order, or a feeder-school/rank-order sequence), so "how far the allocation reached" is really a mix of a rank score and, where relevant, a distance. We currently hold only the most recently published round — 2025 entry, information as of 3 March 2025 — so treat this as a single year's outcome, not a multi-year trend. We'll expand this table to match the Buckinghamshire format once 2023 and 2024 figures are confirmed from a primary Consortium or school source.
