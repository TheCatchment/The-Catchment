Buckinghamshire Council publishes an "Allocation Profile" after every National Offer Day, showing exactly which rule each school's last place was allocated under, and the distance that rule reached. The number only means "you needed to live this close" in years marked plain — those are genuine out-of-catchment Distance cut-offs; years marked c, s or p were decided by catchment, siblings, or an inner priority-area tier instead, so distance was never reached. It moves a lot year to year — sometimes by several miles — so treat any single year as a snapshot, not a guarantee. Historically, out-of-county appeals rarely succeed either: Chiltern Relocation, a local relocation agency, cites a 3% success rate for out-of-county grammar appeals in 2020 — the most recent figure we've found — so a property decision made on the assumption of winning an appeal is a risky one.
