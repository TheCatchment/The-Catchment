Why parents trust The Catchment

Independent

We're parent-built and not affiliated with any school, council or admissions authority. Paid placement may exist one day — but it will never change what we say.

Accurate

Every guide is checked against official council and school sources. If something changes, we update it.

Local

We focus on HA4 and HA5 because that's where families need clarity the most — not generic national advice.

Calm

Admissions can feel high-stakes and urgent. We try to make it feel manageable instead — plain explanations, delivered without drama, so you can think clearly rather than panic.

A note on scope: we currently cover the HA4 and HA5 postcode districts — Ruislip, Eastcote, and the Pinner/Hatch End edge of Harrow. We're expanding carefully, area by area, rather than promising more than we've actually verified.
